What is candle making?

Candle making is the craft of melting wax, adding wicks and optional scents or colours, and shaping them inside containers or molds.

Every Wow Candle is poured by hand, left to cure for several days, and tested before it reaches you, so it burns evenly and smells the way it should.

How we make a candle

  1. Melt the wax

    Heat soy wax in a double boiler to about 75 °C until fully liquid.

  2. Fix the wick

    Stick the wick to the centre of the jar and hold it straight with a clip.

  3. Add colour and scent

    Let the wax cool to about 65 °C, then stir in dye and fragrance oil for two minutes.

  4. Pour

    Pour slowly at about 55 °C to avoid air bubbles and cracks.

  5. Cure

    Leave it undisturbed for 24 hours to set, then 3–7 days for the scent to develop.

  6. Trim and test

    Trim the wick to 5 mm and burn a test candle before packing.
