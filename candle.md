Candle making is the craft of melting wax, adding wicks and optional scents or colors, and shaping them inside containers or molds.


Essential Materials & Tools

• Waxes: Soy wax, paraffin, beeswax, palm wax, or coconut apricot blends.
• Wicks: Cotton (flat or square-braided) or wood wicks sized to the container.
• Additives: Fragrance oils, essential oils, and candle-specific dyes.
• Containers or Molds: Heat-resistant glass jars, metal tins, or silicone/plastic molds.
• Equipment: Double boiler or melting pot, digital scale, kitchen/candle thermometer, wick stickers, and a wick-centering tool (like a clothespin or wick bar).

Purpose & Ambiance
Functional: Used for emergency lighting during power outages or for timekeeping.
Atmospheric: Creates a cozy, calm, romantic, or meditative mood.
Ceremonial: Used for birthdays, religious vigils, memorials, or festive celebrations. 

Scent & Fragrance
Unscented: Clean, subtle, or waxy smell.
Scented: Infused with notes such as floral, woody, citrus, spicy, or sweet aromas that fill a room when burned.
